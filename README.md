## 🌍 What is Eldoria?

Eldoria is a persistent online fantasy world built entirely within Discord. As a player, you begin by establishing your own kingdom in a realm filled with adventure, danger, and opportunity.

**Create & Build** — Found your kingdom, develop its infrastructure, and grow your realm from a humble settlement into a mighty empire.

**Explore & Gather** — Fish in enchanted waters, mine deep beneath the mountains, farm fertile lands, and discover hidden treasures across the realm.

**Heroes & Companions** — Recruit powerful heroes across six distinct classes, raise loyal pets, and build a formidable force to carry your banner into battle.

**Economy & Trade** — Participate in a living economy with banking, markets, auctions, and trade. Every coin earned is a coin earned through your own strategy.

**Alliances & Warfare** — Forge alliances with fellow rulers, wage wars against rival factions, and compete in arenas, tournaments, and epic world events.

**Quests & Glory** — Embark on quests, climb the prestige ranks, unlock achievements, and etch your name into the leaderboard of legends.

Eldoria is designed for long-term progression — there is no shortcut to greatness, only the journey.
